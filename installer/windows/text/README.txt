Thank you for downloading NFL Street Reborn! A mod for NFL Street 2 which adds upgraded graphics, updated rosters, and much more!

Make sure you have:

A legally dumped copy of NFL Street 2 for the GameCube, the USA version of the game!
A controller that you can play the game with!

That's pretty much it! The install process will guide you through everything, but if you need some help, feel free to join our Discord!

FIRST LAUNCH
The installer includes Dolphin, textures, ReShade and a verified Reborn patch. It does not include the original game. On first launch, choose Start fresh and browse to your legally obtained NFL Street 2 (USA) GameCube image, or choose an existing Reborn installation to keep your game and saves.
